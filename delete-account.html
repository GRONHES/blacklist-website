Account Deletion Request

At Blacklist Mobile, we respect your privacy and your control over your personal data.

How to request account deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your Blacklist Mobile account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email to spngamess@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include the following verification details in your message:
    • Display Name: (Your in-game username)
    • User/Account ID: (Found in your in-game profile settings)
    • Registered Email: (The email address used to sign up)

What happens next?

Once we receive your request, we will verify the account ownership. After verification, we will proceed with the permanent deletion of your account and its associated personal data from our servers within 30 days.

Important Note: This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your game progress, purchases, and inventory will be permanently lost. We may retain certain transaction logs for legal and accounting purposes as required by applicable laws.

For more information, please see our Privacy Policy.
