Effective Date: August 14, 2026

These terms apply to the Blacklist Mobile Pre-Registration Reward provided by SPN GAMES for players who pre-register for Blacklist: Online FPS through Google Play.

1. Eligibility

Players who complete pre-registration for Blacklist: Online FPS during the official pre-registration period may be eligible to receive the exclusive pre-registration reward.

  • The reward is available only to eligible users through Google Play's official pre-registration system.
  • Each eligible account can receive the reward only once.
  • The reward cannot be transferred between accounts.

2. Reward Delivery

The pre-registration reward will be delivered after the official release of Blacklist: Online FPS.

Players must install the game and access Blacklist Mobile using the eligible Google Play account in order to receive the reward.

3. Reward Details

The reward is an exclusive in-game digital item created for players who supported Blacklist Mobile before launch.

  • The reward has no cash value.
  • The reward cannot be exchanged for real money.
  • The reward cannot be sold, traded, or transferred.

Players must use a valid account that follows Google Play policies and SPN GAMES rules.

Accounts involved in fraudulent activities, abuse, or violations of game rules may become ineligible for receiving the reward.
